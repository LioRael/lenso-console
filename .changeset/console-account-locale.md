Expose a shared Console locale context, Plugin translation namespaces, lazy catalog loading, and locale-aware date and number formatting. Ordinary authenticated accounts can open personal Settings, persist their language, or follow a global default. Global default changes require the explicit `console.locale.default.manage` Access Control permission and a durable locale-store provider.
